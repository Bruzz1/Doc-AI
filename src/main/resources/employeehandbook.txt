# Employee Handbook

## Company Information
Company Name: Acme Technologies
Founded: 2018
Headquarters: Makati City, Philippines
CEO: Maria Santos

## Working Hours
Employees work from Monday to Friday.
Office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Flexible work arrangements are available for approved employees.

## Leave Policy
- Annual Leave: 15 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Emergency Leave: 3 days per year

Unused annual leave can be carried over for up to 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Password Policy
- Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
- Passwords must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required.

## Travel Policy
Employees traveling for business are eligible for reimbursement.

Maximum hotel allowance:
- Metro Manila: PHP 6,000 per night
- Outside Metro Manila: PHP 4,000 per night

Meal allowance:
